# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01

We only collect the minimal information needed to run the game
(e.g., lobby code, chosen player name, gameplay events). We do not sell
your personal information.

## Data We Process
- Lobby/room codes and membership
- Player display names you type
- Gameplay state (bids, hands won, scores)
- Basic technical logs (IP address, timestamps) for abuse prevention

If you enable analytics/ads in the future, we will update this policy
and disclose the vendors used.

## Cookies/Storage
We use localStorage for rejoining a room (player key) and may use
cookies for session or security. You can clear these in your browser.

## Retention
Room and logs may be deleted periodically or when inactive.
